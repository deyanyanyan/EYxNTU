Proposal for review · LMS Studio · 28 Oct 2026

Course workspace redesign

Today the Course tab asks people to understand the pipeline before they can change their course. This proposal organises it around the course itself: the outline you approve, the lessons you review, and one way to regenerate any part of either, with your own instructions.

The mockups use the Northwind demo course and its real reviewer findings.

What gets in the way today

Numbers 1 to 4 are the points you raised. 5 and 6 came up while building the last features.

1

Two regenerate buttons

“New version” under Plan and “Regenerate” under Generate lessons look alike. One rebuilds the course structure, the other rewrites lesson content.

2

All or nothing

Regeneration covers every lesson at once, and there is no way to say what should change.

3

Flags without a fix

The reviewer marks contradictions and weak quiz options, but nothing turns a flag into a correction.

4

The outline is hidden

Modules and lessons sit inside “Plan the course”, below the learning objectives, so they do not read as the course.

5

The pipeline appears twice

The step cards repeat the Ingest, Plan, Generate, Export stepper in the top bar.

6

Lessons hide the outline

Opening a lesson replaces the tab, so moving between lessons means going back to the list each time.

The idea

Most of the confusion comes from one missing distinction. Changing what the course covers and changing what a lesson says are different jobs, and today they look the same. The redesign gives each job its own place and lets every change start from the thing you are looking at.

Structure and content live apart

The Outline tab decides modules, lessons and objectives. The Lessons tab decides slides and quizzes.

Change things where you see them

A course, module, lesson, slide or question has its own regenerate action, so the scope is never in doubt.

Every change can be undone

Approved content is never overwritten. Fixes arrive as proposed changes, and the previous version stays one click away.

Four stage tabs, starting with the outline

The Course tab becomes four tabs: Sources, Outline, Lessons and Publish. Each tab label carries its stage’s status, which replaces both the step cards and the top-bar stepper. Each tab has one primary action that follows the course’s state: approve the outline, generate lessons, fix flags, export.

Lessons: a list and a reading pane

The Lessons tab keeps the lesson list beside the open lesson. Each lesson shows its state at a glance. Ticking lessons brings up an action bar for that selection, so “regenerate these two” or “fix everything flagged here” takes one step.

One composer for every regeneration

Every regenerate action opens the same small panel. It names the scope it was opened from, takes optional instructions, offers a few common intents as one-tap chips, and states what will be replaced and what will be kept before anything runs. Changing the scope is a choice inside the panel, so nobody has to find a different button.

Fixes arrive as proposed changes

A fix should never quietly rewrite a lesson. Targeted fixes come back as a short list of proposed changes, each showing exactly what moves, with the reviewer’s fresh verdict beside it. Accepting is one click per change or one click for all.

Publish shows what is left

The last tab gathers export, the in-app preview and the export history, and states what is still open before you send a course to an LMS. Open items are links into the Lessons tab, and none of them blocks an export.

Build order

Each phase is usable on its own. Phase 1 is mostly front-end and fixes points 1, 4, 5 and 6 without new generation features.

Phase 1 · Reorganise

smaller
  • Stage tabs with status, replacing the step cards and the stepper
  • Outline tab with modules and lessons first
  • Lessons tab as list plus reading pane
  • One state-driven primary action per tab

Underneath: no new endpoints.

Phase 2 · Targeted regeneration

larger
  • The composer, with scope, instructions and intents
  • Regenerate selected lessons, one slide, or one question
  • Proposed changes with Accept and Keep original
  • Lesson version history with restore

Underneath: lesson generation takes lesson ids and instructions; slide and question regeneration endpoints; previous asset versions kept instead of deleted (one migration).

Phase 3 · Fix and edit

larger
  • Fix and Fix all from reviewer findings, with re-check
  • Edit slide and question text in place
  • Rename, reorder, add and remove lessons; move objectives
  • Approve lessons; compare outline versions

Underneath: instructions built from findings and source passages; re-review of changed assets only; outline and approval endpoints.

Decisions for you

My recommendation is on the right of each.

Replace the step cards and top-bar stepper with stage tabs?

The tabs carry the same status, so nothing is lost, and the Course tab stops being one long page.

Yes

Targeted fixes wait for Accept. Whole-lesson regeneration applies at once, with the previous version one click away.

Editing would create a new outline version automatically, with a compare view, so approval keeps its meaning.
